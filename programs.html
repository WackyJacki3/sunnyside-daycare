Infants · 0–18 months

The Cubby

Slow days, lots of cuddles, sleep when you sleep. The Cubby moves at your baby's pace — we follow your routine, your bottles, your naps.

A typical day

Mostly snuggles, songs, and sensory time. Tummy time on the mat, two stroller walks, and naps on demand. Daily photo updates so you know how it's going.

What's included

  • 1 : 3 educator ratio
  • Bottle feeding & solids
  • Diapers + wipes provided
  • Daily updates with photos
  • Quiet nap room
Toddlers · 18 mo – 3 years

The Sunbeam Room

Little explorers learning big feelings. Pretend play, messy hands, and a thousand discoveries before lunch — with the patience and rhythm toddlers need.

A typical day

Circle time with songs. Free play in stations — kitchen, blocks, art. Two outdoor times. Family-style lunch. Naps. Afternoon adventure outside or in.

What's included

  • 1 : 4 educator ratio
  • Hot lunch + two snacks daily
  • Gentle potty learning support
  • Music, books & art every day
  • Weekly menu shared with parents
Preschool · 3 – 5 years

The Treehouse

Curious minds, ready for kindergarten. Project days, big questions, and a lot of new friends — we focus on confidence, kindness, and curiosity before flashcards.

A typical day

Morning meeting and project intros. Hands-on activities — building, science, art. Outside time or forest school. Family-style lunch, quiet time, and afternoon free play.

What's included

  • 1 : 8 educator ratio
  • Weekly forest school day
  • Early literacy & numeracy
  • Kindergarten-readiness program
  • Field trips & seasonal celebrations

Common questions

Things parents often ask.

If your question isn't here, just drop us a line — we love a good chat.

When can my child move up to the next room?

We transition kids when they're ready — usually around their birthday, but always when we notice they're stretching out of their current room. Moves are gradual, with a few visits to the new room first so it feels familiar.

What's the typical day like?

Predictable but never rigid. Open arrival from 7am, breakfast snack, outside time, hot lunch around 11:30, naps or quiet time, an afternoon snack and creative play, then goodbye hugs from about 5pm.

Do you accommodate food allergies and special diets?

Yes. Our cook works with families to plan around allergies, sensitivities, and dietary preferences. We share menus a week in advance so you always know what's coming.

How often will I hear about my child's day?

Every day. Short notes with photos via our parent app, plus a chat at pickup. The big stuff — a new milestone, a bumped knee, a brand-new friend — gets a more detailed message.

What should we bring on the first day?

A change of clothes (or two), indoor shoes, a labelled water bottle, rain gear or a sun hat depending on the season, and any comfort items your child sleeps with. We provide everything else.
