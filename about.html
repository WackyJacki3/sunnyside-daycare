About

Ten years of warm hellos and hard goodbyes.

Sunnyside started as one family's wish for a daycare that felt more like a home. A decade later, that wish is still our only goal.

Est. 2014 Langley, BC Licensed by Fraser Health
Our story

Built by a family, for families.

In 2014, after struggling to find a daycare that felt warm enough for their own children, the Nguyen family opened Sunnyside's doors to their neighbourhood.

The idea was simple: a place where kids feel genuinely at home, where educators know each child by name and favourite book, and where parents don't have to worry from drop-off to pickup.

Ten years on, we've grown to three rooms and a team of twelve. The same family — and the same feeling — is still at the centre of everything we do.

What we believe

Three things that guide everything.

Not a mission statement on the wall — just how we actually work, every day.

Play is the work

Children learn through doing, creating, pretending, and exploring. We protect time for unstructured play — it's not a break from learning, it is the learning.

Family comes with the child

We partner with parents, not replace them. Daily updates, open-door drop-ins, and a staff who always welcomes a quick call — that's just part of the deal here.

Safe enough to take risks

Safe environments don't mean quiet, still ones. We create space where children can climb, get muddy, and try things that might not work — because that's how confidence grows.
