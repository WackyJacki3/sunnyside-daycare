Programs

Care that fits each little stage.

Three rooms, each shaped around the rhythm of its age group — from snuggly infants to soon-to-be kindergartners.

Infants · 0–18 months

The Cubby

Slow days, lots of cuddles, sleep when you sleep. We follow your routine — naps, bottles, all of it.

  • 1:3 educator ratio
  • Daily updates with photos
  • Sensory play & tummy time
  • Sleep in our quiet nap room
Learn about The Cubby
Toddlers · 18 mo – 3 yrs

The Sunbeam Room

Little explorers learning big feelings. Pretend play, messy hands, and so much joy.

  • 1:4 educator ratio
  • Gentle potty learning
  • Music, movement & stories
  • Two outdoor times daily
Learn about Sunbeams
Preschool · 3 – 5 yrs

Curious minds, ready for kindergarten. Project days, big questions, and lots of new friends.

Our approach

Built around play, the way kids actually learn.

We're not in the business of flashcards. We're in the business of confident, curious humans.

— 01

Play is the work.

Children learn through pretend, blocks, mud, and movement. We follow their lead and stay curious alongside them.

— 02

Outside, every day.

Rain or shine, you'll find us in our garden, on a neighbourhood walk, or stomping in puddles in our boots.

— 03

Nourishment, made fresh.

Hot meals and snacks made on-site by our cook, with menus shared each week. Allergies and preferences welcomed.

A day with us

The rhythm of a sunny day.

Predictable, but never rigid. Here's how a typical day flows — give or take a few extra puddle stomps.

  1. 7:00

    Soft hellos

    Open door, quiet puzzles, and a warm welcome for early arrivals.

  2. 8:30

    Breakfast snack

    Fresh fruit, toast, oats — fuel for a full morning ahead.

  3. 9:30

    Outside time

    Garden, sandbox, scooters, or a walk to the park down the street.

  4. 11:30

    Hot lunch, together

    Family-style at the big table. Pass the bread, please.

  5. 12:30

    Naps & quiet time

    Cozy cots, soft music, and storybooks for those who'd rather rest.

  6. 3:00

    Snack & creative play

    Art carts, dress-up, blocks, and whatever today's curiosity calls for.

  7. 5:00

    Goodbye hugs

    A wave from the window and a story to bring home about the day.
