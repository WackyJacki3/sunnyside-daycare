The rooms

One building, three worlds.

Each room is sized and set up around the developmental needs of the age group inside it.

Infants · 0–18 months

The Cubby

Quiet, soft, and slow. Low lighting, cosy sleep nooks, and mats for tummy time. Designed for naps on demand and lots of one-on-one time with your baby's dedicated educator.

Toddlers · 18 mo – 3 yrs

The Sunbeam Room

Bright, busy, and just a little bit loud. Play kitchens, a building area, a cosy book corner, and enough floor space for the wiggles. The mess is intentional — and part of the fun.

Preschool · 3 – 5 yrs

The Treehouse

Project tables, a science corner, a writing wall, and a quiet nook for when the world gets to be a lot. Sized for exploration and built for kids who are getting ready for big-kid school.

Outside

We're out the door every single day.

Rain or shine — with the right gear, every day is an outdoor day. Our yard has enough going on to keep any kid busy for hours.

  • Raised garden beds the kids help tend
  • Sandbox with scoops, buckets & diggers
  • Tricycle path and open play area
  • Climbing structure and balance beams
  • Shaded patio for hot days
  • Weekly forest walks for The Treehouse
