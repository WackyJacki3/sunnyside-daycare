Enrollment

Let's find the right fit for your family.

Spots are limited but we genuinely love meeting new families. Start with a tour — there's no pressure, just a chat and a walk around the space.

Currently accepting Fall 2026 3 spots open Ages 0 – 5
How it works

Four simple steps.

No lengthy application portals. Just a conversation, a visit, and a whole lot of excitement for what's ahead.

01

Book a tour

Come see the space and meet the team. Bring your child — they're the most important opinion in the room.

02

Short application

A brief form about your child, your schedule, and what you're hoping for. Takes about 10 minutes.

03

Offer or waitlist

If a spot is available, we'll make an offer within a week. If not, we'll add you to our waitlist and keep you updated.

04

Welcome to Sunnyside

A gradual settling-in period so your child builds trust at their own pace before full days begin.
